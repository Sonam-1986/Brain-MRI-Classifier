Pipeline Architecture

How The 5-Stage Segmentation Works

Combining state-of-the-art Deep Convolutional Neural Networks with classical morphological Computer Vision for clinically transparent analysis.

01

Preprocessing & Contrast

Grayscale conversion, CLAHE (Contrast Limited Adaptive Histogram Equalization), and Non-Local Means denoising to enhance subtle intensity variations.

02

Skull Stripping & Brain Mask

Otsu optimal thresholding and morphological closing extract the brain parenchyma while stripping extraneous cranial bone and background noise.

03

K-Means Tissue Separation

Unsupervised 5-cluster K-Means segments Cerebrospinal Fluid (CSF), Gray Matter, White Matter, and anomalous hyper-intense regions.

04

Deep Learning & CAM

Our 4-stage Residual CNN computes Class Activation Maps (CAM) to accurately localize lesions and detect peritumoral vasogenic edema.

05

Multi-Layer Tumor Overlay

Generates high-contrast color overlays distinguishing the core lesion, necrotic centers, and surrounding tissue boundaries with quantitative biomarkers.

About The Classifier

The MRI Brain Tumor Classifier is an AI-assisted diagnostic tool designed to help researchers, clinicians, and medical imaging specialists inspect MRI scans with transparent, explainable computer vision segmentation.

By unifying deep convolutional feature representations with morphological contouring, the system generates verifiable biomarkers including area ratios, hemispheric asymmetry index, and ring enhancement scores.

PyTorch Residual CNN OpenCV Morphological CV Class Activation Mapping (CAM) Otsu Skull Stripping K-Means Tissue Clustering
