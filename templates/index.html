Pipeline Architecture

5-Stage Image Segmentation Pipeline

Our transparent medical computer vision architecture unifies Deep Learning with classical morphological segmentation.

01

Preprocessing & Contrast

Grayscale conversion, CLAHE (Contrast Limited Adaptive Histogram Equalization), and Non-Local Means denoising enhance subtle pixel gradients.

02

Skull Stripping & Brain Mask

Otsu optimal thresholding and morphological closing isolate intracranial brain parenchyma while removing extraneous cranial bone and artifact noise.

03

K-Means Tissue Separation

Unsupervised 5-cluster K-Means segments Cerebrospinal Fluid (CSF), Gray Matter, White Matter, and anomalous hyper-intense regions.

04

Deep Residual CNN & CAM

Our 4-stage Residual CNN computes Class Activation Maps (CAM) to accurately localize lesions and detect peritumoral vasogenic edema.

05

Multi-Layer Tumor Overlay

Generates high-contrast color overlays distinguishing the core lesion, necrotic centers, and surrounding tissue boundaries with quantitative biomarkers.

About The System

AI-assisted medical imaging solution for brain MRI classification and tissue segmentation.

Brain MRI Analyzer & Segmentation Suite

The MRI Brain Tumor Classifier is an AI-assisted diagnostic tool designed to help researchers, clinicians, and medical imaging specialists inspect MRI scans with transparent, explainable computer vision segmentation.

By unifying deep convolutional feature representations with morphological contouring, the system generates verifiable biomarkers including area ratios, hemispheric asymmetry index, vasogenic edema scoring, and ring enhancement analysis.
