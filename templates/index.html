About

MRI Brain Tumor Segmentation & Classification System

6-Stage CV Pipeline

1

Preprocessing

Grayscale conversion, CLAHE contrast enhancement, Gaussian blur noise reduction.

2

Segmentation

Otsu adaptive thresholding, morphological operations (close/open/dilate) for clean binary brain mask.

3

Tissue / Region Separation

Connected components labeling, skull stripping via flood fill, brain tissue isolation.

4

Abnormal Region Detection

Hyperintensity detection (mean + 1.5σ), hemispheric asymmetry scoring, statistical outlier detection.

5

Tumor Region Extraction

Contour detection, convex hull analysis, circularity & solidity features, bounding-box extraction.

6

Classification

Multi-feature weighted scoring (area ratio, intensity contrast, asymmetry, morphology) → Tumor/Normal + confidence score.
